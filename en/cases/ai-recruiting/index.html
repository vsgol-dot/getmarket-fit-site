Case: B2B AI recruiting assistant, pilot to paid conversion from 20% to 34%

Updated November 25, 2026

Result: pilot to paid conversion grew from 20% to 34%.

  • Product: B2B AI assistant for recruiting
  • Format: ongoing product advisory, regular work with the team

How the product works

A recruiter describes a vacancy. The service finds the best matching resumes in the databases, and the recruiter marks the people they are interested in. Then the system chats with the candidates on its own, runs screening and interviews, and returns a final shortlist.

Starting point

The pilot was set up so that for each vacancy the client saw two or three chats and a couple of interviews. That was not enough to feel the value: the result people buy the service for never showed up during the pilot. 20% of clients paid after the pilot.

What we found

We worked out what the main effect for the client is. It is not individual conversations but a ready shortlist of the best matching candidates who have already passed screening.

What we changed

We rebuilt the pilot: one vacancy and two weeks, so the system has time to run all the conversations and screening. At the end of the pilot the client gets that shortlist and sees right away that the service works.

Outcome

Pilot to paid conversion grew from 20% to 34%.
