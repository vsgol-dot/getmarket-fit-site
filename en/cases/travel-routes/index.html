Case: B2C travel routes service, signup conversion from 7% to 12%

Updated November 25, 2026

Result: signup conversion of search traffic grew from 7% to 12%.

  • Product: B2C travel routes service, monetized through a subscription
  • Format: ongoing product advisory, regular work with the team
  • Budget for the changes: minimal

Starting point

There was a lot of traffic, almost all of it from search. People landed on a route page, found out where to go and left. 7% signed up. Without a signup there is no way to keep working with a person, let alone monetize them, so the goal became more signups.

What we did

  1. Mapped the segments inside the platform and how each of them uses the service.
  2. Chose the segment that matters most right now.
  3. Ran about ten interviews with that segment and about as many with the others: their pains, fears, and what they need from the service.
  4. Rebuilt the route pages people land on from search. Each of them became a landing page that explains why to sign up, in the words of the chosen segment.

Outcome

Signup conversion grew from 7% to 12%, 1.7 times, on the same traffic.
