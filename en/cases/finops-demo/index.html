Case: B2B FinOps service, demo to pilot conversion from 30% to 50%

Updated November 25, 2026

Result: demo to pilot conversion grew from about 30% to 50%. After the demo, every second company wanted to try the product.

  • Product: B2B FinOps service that helps companies cut cloud costs
  • Format: one consultation
  • Budget for the changes: none, only the demo script changed

Starting point

Sales went through demos, and about 30% of companies moved from a demo to a pilot. The team walked us through how they ran the demo.

What we found

The demo barely showed the product’s main value. It focused on the technical side: how the service is built and how it works. The person who makes the buying decision never got an answer to their question: what will this give my company.

What we changed

We rebuilt the demo around the value for the ideal customer, specifically for the decision maker. The technical part stayed, but moved to the background.

Outcome

The demo started converting almost twice as well: about half of the companies now move from a demo to a pilot.
