Case: B2C AI photo shoot service, paid conversion from 2.7% to 4%

Updated November 25, 2026

Result: paid conversion grew from 2.7% to 4%. From the same number of signups, almost 1.5 times more paying users.

  • Product: B2C AI photo shoot service, the first photo shoot is free
  • Format: one consultation
  • Timeline: about two weeks to ship the changes and run tests
  • Budget for the changes: minimal, only the paywalls were reworked

Starting point

Traffic was around 10,000 people a day, and part of it was paid. About 80% of signups got as far as creating a free photo shoot and downloading the photos. Only 2.7% paid. The other 77% went through the whole free path and left: these were the people to work with.

What we found

First we looked at conversion at every step. The main leak was not at the entry: 77% of signups got a free result and left without buying anything, even though the service had paid for part of that traffic. One reason was simple: along the way they never saw a single paywall. The service offered nothing to the people who were leaving.

What we changed

We changed the user flow and started showing paywalls at several points inside the creation of the first, free photo shoot. People now see the offer while they are already getting value, not after they have downloaded the photos and left. After launch we ran several more tests of placements and wording.

Outcome

The share of paying users grew from 2.7% to 4%. No new traffic: one consultation found where the money was leaking, and the team did the rest in two weeks.
